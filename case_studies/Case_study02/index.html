SETTING THE SCENE

✦

The puzzle screen is where players spend most of their time.

DESIGN BRIEF

How can we enhance the clarity, consistency & usability of key interactive elements on the game board, ensuring players can understand & engage effortlessly? Envision UI that guides naturally through the puzzle screen improving Session Length.

PROBLEM SPACE

✦

Every friction point on the puzzle screen multiplies

In order to know the existing friction points on the screen & area of opportunities which can help to enhance the gameplay experience, the puzzle needed a systematic UX audit.

01

Readability & Tap Accuracy

Tiles were small, causing mistaps — especially for older players. The rack was undersized, making the “snap into place” feel weak. Multiple daily support tickets requesting bigger tiles.

Small tiles Mistaps Accessibility
02

Visual Hierarchy Breakdown

Power-ups blended with the tiles (both square). The rack lacked distinction from the board. Jewel notifs were oversized, dominating the icon space.

No differentiation Unclear hierarchy Visual noise
03

Lack of Gameplay Feedback

No gratification on making matches. No rack fill feedback. Silent interactions made the game feel unresponsive. Players had no sense on momentum or delight during active gameplay.

No feedback Silent UI Low engagement
04

UI Inconsistency

Extra slot looked nothing like other powerups despite functioning as one. The w2e icon was visually inconsistent with the home screen version. Too many similar tones of same colour family.

Inconsistent elements Visual clutter Disjointed palette

THE CHANGE & UX REASONING

✦

Every change backed by design principles

Validated through competitive analysis of top-performing tile-match games to benchmark UI patterns and inform design decisions.

A

Visual Changes

Making the core gameplay elements bigger, consistent, and more intuitive.

01 Tile Scaling & Base Color

Shifted from square to slightly rectangular tiles to increase tile size within the existing board constraints, without altering the grid structure. Changing tile base colour for better visibility.

WHY THIS WORKS
  • Larger tiles → easier to scan, recognise, & decide which match to make.
  • Off-white base → reduce harsh contrast, improving visual comfort across varying brightness levels.
  • Fewer mistaps → less frustration, which can positively impact session length and return rate.

NEW

02 Rack System Optimisation

Refined the rack by increasing its size, repositioning it above the board, and introducing a holder with a subtle background tint. Together, these changes improve visibility, reinforce spatial hierarchy, and mimics the tactile behaviour of physical tile games.

WHY THIS WORKS
  • Stronger interaction feedback → Larger rack and holder enhance the “snap” effect, simulating real-world tile placement.
  • Clear visual hierarchy → Top-down structure (Rack → Board → Controls) aligns with natural scanning patterns and decision flow.
  • Improved state visibility → Background tint and defined slots make availability instantly recognizable.
  • Better object distinction → Holder creates separation between rack tiles and board tiles, reducing visual ambiguity.
  • Scales with complexity → Maintains clarity even in dense or vertically extended levels.
OLD
NEW

03 Power up Visibility & Accessibility

Redesigned power-ups with circular holders, neutral tones, and increased size, while repositioning them closer to the board for stronger spatial anchoring. These changes establish power-ups as distinct, accessible tools within the gameplay environment.

WHY THIS WORKS
  • Clear visual differentiation → Circular form and neutral tones separate power-ups from multi-colored tiles, reducing visual competition.
  • Faster recognition → Distinct shape and styling enable quick identification during gameplay.
  • Improved accessibility → Larger size and reduced distance to the board enhance tap accuracy (Fitts’s Law).
  • Stronger spatial anchoring → Elevated and closer placement reinforces power-ups as persistent UI elements, not part of the board.
OLD
NEW

04 System Consistency & Action Clarity

Standardised extra slot and watch-to-earn elements by aligning their visual language, iconography, and labelling with the broader UI system. The extra slot was integrated into the power-up system, while the reward CTA was simplified with a clearer “Free” cue and aligned with rest of the UI.

WHY THIS WORKS
  • Consistent mental models → Similar functions now share a unified visual language (Law of Uniform Connectedness), reducing ambiguity.
  • Improved discoverability → Players can instantly identify the extra slot as part of the power-up system.
  • Stronger conversion cues → “Free” messaging is more direct and motivating than numeric rewards.
  • Reduced visual noise → Fewer competing colors and styles create a cleaner, more scannable interface.
OLD
NEW

05 Jewel Notification Refinement

Compared to competitor games, the jewel notification badges on our power-ups were noticeably larger and dominated the icon space. The badge is a secondary signal; it should inform, not overpower.

WHY THIS WORKS
  • Improved visual hierarchy → Smaller badge size ensures the power-up remains the primary focus.
  • Better visibility → Red colour universally recognised as a notification indicator across platforms, ensuring visibility regardless of background colour.
  • Clearer system states → Enhanced disabled (no-internet) state with improved iconography to communicate unavailability without ambiguity.

REFLECTION

✦

Learnings from the Project

Small, principled changes compound

No single change drives impact in isolation. Consistent improvements across usability, clarity, and feedback can collectively lead to meaningful gains in engagement. Well-designed systems outperform isolated optimizations.

Ship one change at a time

Shipping changes individually allowed us to measure the impact of each change in isolation. Measuring one variable at a time ensures reliable insights and reduces ambiguity in decision-making.

Sometimes constraints drive better solutions

Design limitations often lead to more thoughtful and efficient solutions. Working within constraints encourages prioritization and leads to outcomes that are both practical and scalable.

Validate assumptions with data

Intuitive design decisions don’t always translate to better outcomes. Continuous testing and validation are critical to ensure that solutions align with actual user behaviour.

KEY TAKEAWAY

In casual gaming, the puzzle screen IS the product. Every pixel matters — not for aesthetics, but for how effortlessly a player can understand and engage with the game.

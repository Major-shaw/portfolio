Daily/Weekly Quest System

Lighthouse for Retention

A daily task system designed to increase multi-day retention by giving players clear daily goals and a rewarding weekly progression arc.

Timeline June — July 2025
Role Product Designer
Platform iOS & Android

TL;DR

Designed a daily quest system for a 500K+ DAU mobile game to address 67% Day 2 retention drop. Through user research and data analysis, identified that players lacked motivation to return daily. Solution delivered +5% D7 retention and +6% level views.

Overview

Setting the Scene

We saw a clear drop in player retention after Day 1. A lot of players were not returning the next day (Rolling Retention).
To understand WHY, we analysed a week of player behaviour data and reviewed 7–8 play test sessions, allowing us to align ultimate goal and design decisions with real player needs.

Key Insights & What We Analysed

Key Insights

A large portion of week-2 players lack a reason to come back daily.

Engagement compounds with each additional return.

A small retained segment delivers higher engagement.

What We Analysed

Need an appointment mechanic that gives players a clear, low-effort reason to return every day, without increasing cognitive or gameplay complexity.

The system need to scale across player types, supporting quick, casual sessions while also allowing highly engaged players to progress without artificial caps.

Focus should be on repeat activation, ensuring that players experience visible progress and rewards within their first few returns to reinforce the habit loop.

04Hypothesis

Hypothesis

After going through both qualitative & quantitative data, I came up with the hypothesis that —

The game lacks a clear system to encourage daily returns. This might be due to limited daily motivation, lack of clear progression goals, repetitive content or external factors like time constraints. Improving perceived purpose in daily sessions should lead to stronger multi-day retention and engagement.

Asking the Right Questions

Based on our hypothesis, we need to identify opportunities that could encourage daily returns, make progress feel meaningful, and introduce freshness into recurring play sessions.

How might we ignite Daily Motivation?

Giving player reason to return.

How might we visualise Forward Momentum?

Making progress, goals, & next steps clear, fast, and motivating.

How might we inject Variety & Novelty?

Reducing boredom through delight moments, freshness, & content variation.

Feature Mechanics: Quest Centre

Objective: Introduce daily/weekly quest system


The mechanic uses a progression loop over a longer duration. Players receive 9 Daily Quests, varying from low-effort to high-engagement actions, & objectives span across multiple game modes, encouraging broader feature discovery without forcing it.


Every completed quest grants specific currency that fills a global progression bar. Layered Rewards: A tiered "Nursery" bar provides instant gratification with 3 intermediate milestones which sinks with weekly progress bar. This 6-7 day cycle was introduced to provide a consistent engagement hook that complements our existing 1-3 day short-form events.

Reward Mechanics

Rewards

To fix the "unrewarding" feeling. Every day task & multi-day engagement.

Short-term Goals

To fix session boredom. Timed challenges.

Reset Timers

To trigger "Fear of Missing Out" (FOMO) and drive return sessions.

06Flows

User Flows

For the flows I broke the flow into 3 phases: Learning phase, Quest collection phase, and Reward grant flow.

Phase 1 — FTUE (First Time User Experience) / Learning Phase

  • Player cleared the level
  • Toaster appears on outro screen (feedback — player has completed a quest)
  • Player comes to Home Screen
  • FTUE on HS, with quest icon in claim state
  • Player taps on quest icon
  • Weekly Quest pop up appears
  • Red Jewel icon to indicate number of unclaimed rewards
  • Player taps on claim CTA
  • Reward grant CTA initiates

Edge case: player can complete multiple quests at a time.

Phase 2 — Regular Flow

  • Player cleared the level
  • Toaster appears on outro screen
  • Player comes to Home Screen
  • Quest icon in claim state with jewel
  • Player taps on quest icon
  • Weekly Quest pop up appears
  • Red Jewel icon to indicate number of unclaimed rewards
  • Player taps on claim CTA
  • Reward grant CTA initiates
  • Player quit or kills the pop up without claiming the reward (alternative path)

Phase 3 — Reward Grant Sequence

  • Player taps on ‘Claim’
  • Currency flies to Weekly Progress Bar
  • Quest Card state changes — ‘completed’ state
  • Plant state updated. Sheen/shake animations on milestone and/or daily reward if achieved
  • Reward grant starts after plant state is updated
  • Gift Box animation — reward grant screen same as DC grant
  • Timer for Today’s Tasks changed. Tick mark appears in place of Daily reward
  • Daily reward animation — fly like GT grant
  • Gift box in weekly bar replaced with ‘tick’. Target in holder updated. Daily reward progress updated
  • Player taps on ‘Collect’

All interactions are disabled after tap on ‘Claim’ till grant sequence is over.
